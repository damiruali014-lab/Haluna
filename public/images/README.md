# Product images

Drop these files here, then set the matching path in `src/data/products.ts`
(`image` on a product or shade) or `src/data/site.ts` (`heroImage`, `ugc.poster`).
Until a path is set, the site renders a CSS/SVG bottle placeholder.

| File | Size (px) | Ratio | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hero.webp | 1600 × 1280 | 5:4 | Home hero |
| fluid-spf50.webp | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 | Daily Fluid SPF 50 |
| tint-spf30-light.webp | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 | Glow Tint SPF 30, Light |
| tint-spf30-medium.webp | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 | Glow Tint SPF 30, Medium |
| tint-spf30-deep.webp | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 | Glow Tint SPF 30, Deep |
| body-mist.webp | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 | Body Mist SPF 50 |
| ugc-poster.webp | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Poster frame for the review video |

Keep the product centred with generous margins: cards crop to 5:4 on phones
and the product page shows 1:1 on tablet/desktop. Export WebP at quality ~80,
aim for under 200 KB each.
